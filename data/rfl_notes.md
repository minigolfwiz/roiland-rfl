## League Office case system — BUILT (Dan, 27 Oct 2026; applies from the 2044 season; the 27 earlier 2043 rulings were backfilled as precedent)
- **Code:** `rfl/case_catalog.py` (every case type with its pool of facts and sentence templates), `rfl/cases.py` (engine: subjects, fact rolls, severity 1-10, option ladders, precedent, probation, appeals, storylines, effects), `rfl/league_office.py` (ballot/apply wrappers + Players of the Week), `rfl/build_league_office.py` (page: severity bar, multi-paragraph facts, "how we got here", precedent, open matters). State lives in `S['office']`: `history` (all rulings), `pending` (hidden storyline data for the current ballot), `stories` (open storylines with hidden truths), `due` (follow-ups), `sigs` (uniqueness fingerprints). Hidden truths are never put in the ballot file or on the page. The ballot CLI now saves the state (it mutates it).
- **Uniqueness:** each case rolls a subject plus a handful of facts from its type's pool; every fact moves the severity; the option ladder is generated around that severity, so similar cases differ in facts, severity and menu. A fingerprint (type, subject, opener, fact set, severity) is checked against all earlier cases. Tested on ~600 simulated cases: zero duplicate texts.
- **Types:** minor (late hit, defenseless hit, helmet, taunting, gesture/fans, coach sideline, comments, social post, missed media, equipment, fan incident), moderate (flagrant ejection, targeting with a real injury from that game's injury list, fight, bench clearing, bounty and hazing complaints), serious (PED incl. masking/second offense, DUI incl. crash, gambling, firearm, misdemeanor assault, reckless driving), major = felony (domestic violence, assault, sexual assault allegation in clinical terms, DUI with injury, trafficking, weapons, burglary, fraud; nothing involving minors), club (illegal filming, injury report, draft leak, owner comments, facility, executive conduct, tampering, cap side payments). Club rate is now 0.05 a week (was 0.02) because the club tier is much wider; other rates unchanged. Follow-ups use up that tier's weekly draw; appeals and reviews come on top.
- **Felony policy (Dan: much harsher than the NFL):** stage 1 options are no action / exempt list / indefinite (review after 20 or 40 games). A hidden evidence strength, court outcome (convicted/plea/dropped) and an independent league finding are rolled at case creation. Stage 2 (3-6 weeks later) reports the court result and the league's own finding; options run from permanent ban to reinstatement. Suggested: convicted -> permanent ban (domestic, assault, sexual assault, trafficking) or indefinite 40; plea -> indefinite 40; dropped but substantiated -> 16 games + program + probation or indefinite 20; dropped and not substantiated -> 4 games or reinstatement.
- **Storylines:** investigations (bounty, hazing, tampering, cap, gambling) open at stage 1 and return 2-4 weeks later as Findings with a hidden truth (substantiated / partial / unfounded); misdemeanor assault gets a court result. Indefinite suspensions get a Reinstatement review once the minimum games pass (facts: program, evaluator, incidents). Rulings with teeth can draw an appeal next week (grounds: procedural, new evidence, proportion, mitigation); options uphold / reduce / rescind. Hannelore Langshaw's old indefinite suspension was migrated (review due 2044 week 12).
- **Real effects:** suspensions (`inj` kind `suspended`; games past the end of a season carry into the next one), `exempt list` and `suspended indefinitely` holds (never healed by `season.heal` or the offseason; end only by a ruling), permanent ban (status retired, `banned` dict, removed from the roster), conduct program (conduct trait nudge, later cases get a small mitigation), one-year probation (later cases +1 severity), coach off the sideline (`club['coach_out']`, runs down in `season.heal`; `game.COACH_OUT_DIP` 0.14 costs about 1 point of margin / 3-4% win probability, measured), pick forfeitures queued in `S['pick_penalties']` (NOT yet consumed by `draft.py`: wire `apply_pick_penalties` when the 2044 draft is built), trait marks scaled via `DISC.mark_case`.
- **Still open:** `draft.py` must consume `S['pick_penalties']` and its `YEAR` is hard-coded to 2043; the page text mentions precedent only where >=1 close earlier ruling exists (the first season has little).

## Discipline traits — BUILT (Dan, 27 Oct 2026; applies from the 2043 offseason on; no 2043 result was touched)
- **Hidden, never shown.** Dan wants the numbers invisible to him: only narrative (flags, fines, how a player behaves) may appear anywhere. Nothing in the pages reads these fields; keep it that way. Never cite a trait value in a recap, preview, case text or write-up.
- **Two 1-100 traits per player, higher = better behaved, league mean ~50, SD ~15:** `disc` (on-field: drives in-game penalties and the personal-foul side of cases) and `cond` (off-field conduct: drives League Office cases). They share one latent temperament (correlation ~0.47) so the same names do not top both lists (60 most-flagged vs 60 most-in-cases overlap ~4). `dgr` = the player's own yearly development rate. Coaches carry `disc` (team-wide baseline; also a small culture effect on their players' conduct drift, +-0.5 a year at the extremes).
- **Where:** `rfl/discipline.py` (generation, norms, drift, League Office weights, `mark_case`). Generation is deterministic from name + birth year (`ensure_traits(S)` is idempotent; existing state was migrated and saved). New players: `engine.gen_player` and `engine.coach_profile` add traits; rookies in `draft.add_rookie` use `trait_for_rookie`, which takes whatever the college sim supplies in the pool file under `disc`, `cond`, `discipline` or `conduct` (1-100; if only an on-field number comes in the other is derived from it) and generates anything missing. Dan said he will add discipline to the college players himself.
- **Yearly drift (`offseason.progress` -> `discipline.progress_traits`, own random stream):** most players mature at their own pace (about +1.7 a year at 50, diminishing near the top, tapering after 31, with noise); ~8% carry a decline arc (negative `dgr`, slow slide, ~-8 over 8 years). Serious League Office rulings usually dent conduct, occasionally a wake-up call instead (`mark_case`, called from `league_office.apply`).
- **Penalties (new; the old engine had none):** `game.py` rolls flags before each scrimmage snap from a separate random stream keyed on game seed + situation (switch `PENALTIES` off and the old engine is reproduced exactly: 300/300 games identical). A flag nullifies the snap (no play stats), moves the ball (half-the-distance rule), and replays the down or awards a first (`drive`). Types: false start, offensive holding, illegal formation/shift, delay of game, offside/neutral zone, defensive holding/illegal contact, pass interference (spot foul), roughing the passer, unnecessary roughness (both sides). Special-teams penalties are not modelled. Results carry `pens` (side, team, q, t at the snap, type, pid, name, pos, yds, down, to_go, af); player stats gain `pen` and `pen_yds`; they flow into the season totals. The Season page does not show flags yet (it ignores unknown fields); `weekly.py` prints a flag digest for the recap.
- **Who is flagged / how often:** offender chosen within the on-field unit by position share x exp(0.35 x z) (`BETA_SEL`), z = how far below the league average a player's `disc` sits, capped at 2.5 SD; team rate scaled by the unit average (`BETA_TEAM` .10) and the coach (`BETA_COACH` .05), all normalised against the league so the traits move who and which team, not the league-wide count. Personal fouls use a 60/40 blend of `disc` and `cond`.
- **Calibration (6000 games a config, same matchups; `rfl/calibrate_discipline.py N`):** 5.3 accepted flags and 40 penalty yards per team-game; points 22.2 vs 22.1 with the old engine, yards 363 vs 366 (a tiny `PEN_ADJ` offset in completion probability and run yards pays back what nullified snaps cost; flags alone had cost ~0.4 points); traits on vs off: 5.34 vs 5.34 flags, scoring unchanged. Best vs worst team ~1.3x; worst-quintile starters draw ~2.2x the flags of the best, no extreme outliers. If PEN_SCALE or a rate is changed, rerun the calibration.
- **League Office:** case odds tilt by hidden traits (`lo_weight`, capped x0.35 to x3, so clean players keep a real chance): off-field cases (comments, equipment, PED, DUI, firearm, criminal) follow `cond`; on-field conduct cases follow a 50/50 blend. On-field cases (late hit, taunting, defenseless, helmet, flagrant) are now anchored to personal fouls actually flagged in that week's games, with the quarter and clock in the text and a "his Nth flag of the season" note at 6 or more; if nothing was flagged no on-field case is invented. Weekly case counts and tiers are unchanged (Poisson rates in `RATES`).
- **Not done:** flags are not yet on the Season page box scores; discipline does not feed benching or form; no discipline in narrative beyond counts and flags.
