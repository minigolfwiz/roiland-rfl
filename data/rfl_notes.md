- Playoffs: 8 per conference, seeded purely by record (division winners auto-qualify), reseeded, higher seed hosts; Mega Bowl at a rotating neutral site (`claude/rfl_mega_bowl_sites.json`). "First round", not "wild card". Ties allowed in the regular season (NFL OT rules).
- Tiebreakers (`season.rank`): win pct (tie = half), head-to-head among tied clubs, division record, conference record, point differential, points for, name. The head-to-head step did not work until 23 Oct 2026 (a `list.sort` emptied the group while keys were computed); fixed with a tuple copy. Seeds were rerun then, no game results changed.
- Overtime rule fix (23 Oct 2026, Dan): after a walk-off touchdown in OT there is no extra point (flagged `noxp` on the play; the Season page does not add the +1). Applies to the Bearcats' 33–27 conference final win as well. Three earlier games were corrected by hand: week 5 Beetles 29–Topcats 23, week 6 Dragons 19–Rapids 13, week 6 Brass Tigers 19–Beetles 13.
- Schedule format: 18 games / 20 weeks, division 2x, two from each other own-conference division (1H/1A), one from each opposing division (2H/2A). Rotation formula in `claude/rfl_matchups.py` (2041 = year 0). Dan places games in the Schedule Office and picks TNF/SNF/MNF in the Broadcast Desk (week 20: no MNF, two SNF).
- **Prime-time flex rule (Dan, 23 Oct 2026): in certain dire circumstances, Sunday nights from week 15 on can be switched for another Sunday game if the league deems that game not worth the primetime slot.** Implemented as `season.flex_check`, run automatically before each week ≥ 15: if both SNF clubs are under .500 and at least one is effectively out of its division race, the game swaps slots with the best Sunday game between two winning clubs (division games preferred). Every flex is logged in `season.flexes` and printed by the runner — tell Dan whenever one happens. 2042 had one: week 18, Topcats at Brass Tigers flexed out for Raptors at Dales.
- History 1951–2041 (compact) in `claude/rfl_history_1951_2040.json` (name unchanged; it includes 2041 and an `offseason_2042` block). **Corrected 23 Oct 2026:** club all-time W-L-T, playoff trips, division titles and the titles/conference-title lists had counted 2041 twice; they now equal the sum of the season records through 2041. Coaching/GM history in `claude/rfl_coaching.json`.

## Engine (rebuilt) — `claude/code/`
- `rfl_engine.py`: player/club dicts, cap (2042 $309.5M), market value, GM/coach profiles (deterministic from name), depth charts, strength, depth-player generator, `compact_state`/`expand_state` for the project copy.
- `rfl_draft.py`: need-based draft (GM eye/aggression/bias, QB premium when needed, K/P discounted), UDFA signings, camp cuts with minimums. `python3 rfl/draft.py 2042` reads `draft/pool_<year>.txt` (pipe-delimited compact pool).
- `rfl_rebuild_rosters.py`: one-off reconstruction from Archive data.
- `rfl_game.py` + `rfl_season.py`: drive-by-drive engine (calibrated: ~22.5 ppg, 64% completions, 7.4 ypa, 4.3 ypc, ~2.3 sacks and ~0.8 INT per team-game, ~250 injuries a season, ~2% ties), tiebreakers, playoffs, awards (regular season only), flex check. `rfl_weekly.py` (one-command week), `rfl_build_page.py` builds the Season page by splicing data into the published page template (re-downloadable any time). `rfl_matchups.py` reproduces 2041 exactly; `rfl_build_office.py` / `rfl_build_desk.py` rebuild the Schedule Office / Broadcast Desk for a year from the published pages.
- Season flow: `python3 rfl/weekly.py N` (or `season.py week N`) → republish the RFL 2042 Season page → `write_project_state` → `claude/rfl_state_2042.z64` after every week and playoff round (and `claude/code/` whenever a script changes) → repo commit and push. Never re-run a played week.
