Letterforms, not mascots

Each program's initials set in a typeface chosen from what its mascot actually is — blackletter for the ghosts and demons, slab for the livestock and the locomotives, a space face for the cosmonauts — inside a shape that belongs to its conference. Nothing is drawn by hand, so this covers all 369 schools.

How the typeface gets picked

One thing I could not check. The fonts load from Google, which this machine cannot reach, so I have seen the layout but not the actual typefaces. Every monogram measures itself once the fonts arrive and scales to fit its badge, so the letters stay centred and correctly sized whatever metrics each face turns out to have — but if a particular face reads wrong to you, that is the one thing here I could not judge for myself.
